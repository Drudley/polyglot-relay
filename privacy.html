Polyglot Relay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Polyglot Relay (the "Bot") and its website process, why we process it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Niklas Bergström, a sole trader (enskild näringsidkare) based in Umeå, Sweden. Contact for privacy questions and requests: drudleybot+polyglot@mail.grokbot.com

2. The short version

  • We do not store the content of your messages. Message text is held in memory only for the few seconds it takes to translate it and post the translation. It is never written to our database or logs.
  • We store only what the Bot needs to work: Discord IDs (for servers, channels, messages, and webhooks), server settings, glossary terms, and monthly character counts.
  • Translation runs on a LibreTranslate engine that we host ourselves in Sweden. It does not send your text to any third party.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Discord. We never see your card details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Glossary terms are written by server admins. Please don't put personal data in them that isn't needed.

4. Who processes your data

  • Discord (Discord Inc. and its affiliates) delivers all messages and commands to the Bot, and handles Premium purchases, billing, and refunds. Discord acts as an independent controller under its own Privacy Policy (discord.com/privacy). We receive only an entitlement record showing that a server has Premium. We do not receive payment details.
  • Self-hosted translation engine. LibreTranslate runs on our own equipment in Sweden, together with the Bot and its database. No third party gets access to the text you translate.
  • AI translation provider (Premium only, optional, not active today). In the future, Premium may use an external AI translation provider as our sub-processor (processor under GDPR art. 28). Before we turn this on, we will name the provider, its location, and the transfer safeguards it uses in this policy, at least 14 days in advance. If the provider is outside the EU/EEA, the transfer will rely on an adequacy decision or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The provider will be bound not to use the text to train its models. Free-plan translation will keep using our self-hosted engine.
  • Website hosting. The Polyglot Relay website is a static site. It uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub Inc.) processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and to protect against abuse, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

We do not sell or share your personal data with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you,
  • have it corrected,
  • have it deleted,
  • restrict its processing,
  • receive it in a portable format (data portability),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To use these rights, email drudleybot+polyglot@mail.grokbot.com and include your Discord user ID or server ID. We will reply within one month.

You can also stop the processing yourself:

  • Server admins: remove the Bot from the server, or remove channel pairs with /autotranslate.
  • Users with a personal install: remove the app under User Settings → Authorized Apps.

Note that most of what we store is linked to servers and channels, not to individual people. Message text is never stored.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se, or with the supervisory authority in your own EU country.

6. Security

  • The Bot, its database, and the translation engine run in isolated containers. None of them is exposed directly to the internet.
  • Access is limited to the operator.
  • The Bot's credentials are stored with restricted file permissions.
  • Webhook tokens are used only to post translations in the channel they belong to.

7. Automated decisions and children

The Bot does not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

The Bot is not directed at children. You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement in your country to use Discord and the Bot.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on the Polyglot Relay website with a new "Last updated" date. If the change is material, for example adding a new sub-processor, we will announce it at least 14 days in advance on the website and in the Bot's /help command.
